Cadence

A timer built around the billing week.

Track time against a project. Every week, Cadence groups it into a total you can check off once you've sent the invoice, so what's billed and what isn't is never a guess.

$ curl -fsSL https://cadence.peaklab.ai/install.sh | bash

Paste into Terminal. Installs to /Applications and launches Cadence.
